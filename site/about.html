Our mission

Proactive, personal, measurable care

Too many health problems are discovered only when a patient arrives in the emergency room. Warning signs were there – a rising blood pressure, a missed medication, a ride that never came – but no one saw them in time.

We help care teams see those signs early, reach patients quickly, and follow through until each concern is resolved. Better for patients, easier for clinicians, and lower in total cost of care.

Vaishnavi Gudupalli is a healthcare quality, patient safety, and regulatory compliance leader with multidisciplinary expertise spanning healthcare administration, clinical quality improvement, health informatics, and artificial intelligence.

She currently serves as Chief Health Disparities Officer and Director of Quality, Performance Improvement, and Risk, with additional responsibilities in Compliance and Privacy. Her professional experience spans substance use disorder treatment, behavioral health, federally qualified health centers, acute care, long-term care, and ambulatory healthcare settings.

With a Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD), a Master’s in Health Informatics, and a Master’s in Computer Science specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Vaishnavi brings a unique combination of clinical knowledge, healthcare operations, regulatory expertise, and technical innovation.

Her areas of expertise include enterprise risk management, patient safety, quality assurance, performance improvement, healthcare accreditation, regulatory compliance, HIPAA, 42 CFR Part 2, healthcare data analytics, and AI-driven quality improvement.

Beyond her leadership responsibilities, Vaishnavi is actively involved in healthcare research, scientific peer review, professional advisory activities, and academic contributions. Her work focuses on advancing patient safety, improving healthcare outcomes, strengthening organizational accountability, and exploring innovative applications of AI in healthcare.

She is passionate about bridging the gap between clinical practice, technology, and quality science to build safer, smarter, and more effective healthcare systems.
