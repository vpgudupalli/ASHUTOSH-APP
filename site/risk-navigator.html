How it works

A closed loop, from check-in to resolution

Patient checks in

Invited patients answer simple questions on their phone – whenever something changes.

Risk is scored

Validated tools feed a transparent score and a clear tier with a response window.

Team is alerted

The assigned provider, NP or case manager sees it live – urgent cases first.

Follow up & close

Every call, message and outcome is logged until the issue is resolved.

Patient experience

Simple enough for anyone

Big buttons, plain language, and real screening questions – patients never need to know their own "scores".

  • ✓Vitals: blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen, temperature, blood sugar, A1c, weight
  • ✓Conditions, medications and how they take them
  • ✓ER visits, hospital stays, falls and daily-living needs
  • ✓Mood (PHQ-9), anxiety (GAD-7), alcohol (AUDIT-C), pain (PEG)
  • ✓Sexual health and social needs – housing, food, transport, safety
  • ✓Disabilities and preferred contact method, so teams reach patients the right way
Evidence-based risk tiers

Five clear tiers, each with a response window

Each area of the score is capped, so checking many boxes in one area can't inflate risk on its own. Missing answers never add risk. Safety findings – like emergency symptoms or thoughts of self-harm – escalate immediately.

Built on PHQ-9, GAD-7, AUDIT-C, PEG, ACC/AHA blood pressure categories, ADA diabetes targets, Charlson comorbidity and LACE readmission concepts, Katz ADLs, CDC STEADI and the CMS AHC social needs screen. Clinical decision support – not a substitute for clinical judgment.

Frequently asked questions

Do patients need to download an app?

No. It works in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

How do patients get access?

Your team adds the patient and sends a one-time invite code. The patient creates an account with any email, verified by date of birth.

Does it work with our EMR?

Risk Navigator exposes a standard HL7 FHIR R4 API, and every check-in can be printed to PDF for chart upload.

Is it a replacement for clinical judgment?

No. It's decision support that helps your team prioritize. Scoring should be calibrated to your population with your clinical leadership.

Is it for emergencies?

No. Patients are always told to call 911 (or 988 for a mental health crisis) in an emergency.
