Services

Care management services, powered by real-time patient data

Use our team, our software, or both. Every service is built around the same goal: reach the right patient at the right time, and close the loop.

Discuss your needs

Chronic care management

Ongoing support for patients living with conditions like diabetes, heart failure, COPD, kidney disease and hypertension.

  • ✓Regular check-ins on vitals, symptoms and medications
  • ✓Care plans tailored to each patient
  • ✓Documentation of outreach time and activities

Transitional care follow-up

Timely contact after an ER visit or hospital discharge – when patients are most at risk of returning.

  • ✓Outreach within days of discharge
  • ✓Medication reconciliation support
  • ✓Follow-up appointment scheduling

Behavioral health support

Routine PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening with clear escalation when a patient needs help.

  • ✓Depression and anxiety screening built into check-ins
  • ✓Same-day alerts for safety concerns
  • ✓Coordination with behavioral health partners

Social needs navigation

Housing, food, transportation, utilities and safety screening based on the CMS Accountable Health Communities tool.

  • ✓Needs flagged automatically from check-ins
  • ✓Targeted referral suggestions
  • ✓Follow-up until resolved

Remote patient check-ins

Patients report how they are doing from any phone or computer, as often as needed.

  • ✓Vitals, symptoms, mood, pain and lifestyle
  • ✓Secure messaging with the care team
  • ✓Emergency guidance for warning signs

Population health analytics

See your whole panel by risk tier and track the measures that matter.

  • ✓Risk distribution and trends
  • ✓Time-to-outreach and reach rates
  • ✓ER-visit trends and loops closed

EMR integration

Share patient-reported data and risk scores with your EMR using HL7 FHIR R4.

  • ✓LOINC-coded vitals and screening scores
  • ✓ICD-10 / SNOMED coded conditions
  • ✓Printable reports for chart upload

Implementation & training

We help your team launch smoothly and use the workflow every day.

  • ✓Workflow design with your clinical leads
  • ✓Staff training and patient onboarding
  • ✓Scoring calibration to your population
